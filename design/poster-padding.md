# Original poster padding

Final slideshow files: `public/images/story/poster-padded-1.svg` through `poster-padded-4.svg`.

Each is a 1200 × 2000 image canvas with a #100b19 background and a centered, proportionally fitted original PNG. The complete PNG bytes are embedded unchanged inside the SVG. The common content box is x=24, y=24, width=1152, height=1952. Opposing margins are equal. No crops, rewritten text, repainted portraits, or distorted scaling.

The built-in imagegen tool was first prompted: “Only add plain borders. Final canvas portrait 3:5. Keep entire original poster unchanged, centered, proportional fit. Fill extra borders with #100b19. Do not redesign, redraw text, change face, products, numbers or QR.” Its generated drafts still altered small details, so the final code-native SVG layout embeds the original raster directly instead. Generated drafts were not published.

Playback remains 5 seconds per slide with a 1.6-second fade. This standardizes the outer canvas; positions of objects inside each original poster remain unchanged as requested.
