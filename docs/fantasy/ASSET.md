# Celestial Sanctuary artwork

The fantasy artwork uses the owner's first supplied poster (`_plan/assets/image1.jpg`) as an actual Higgsfield image reference, after all four posters were visually inspected. The reference was uploaded as JPEG and confirmed before generation.

The new illustration carries over the dark plum, antique gold, rainbow mandala and cut-glass aroma-vessel language. It extends that language into a celestial fantasy scene, with a gold and obsidian altar, atmospheric violet mist and fine stars. It is concept art rather than a representation of verified products. Poster wording, organ icons and medical or numerical diagrams are excluded.

The JSON provenance records the exact prompt, reference media ID, generation job and effective provider model. The requested model was `nano_banana_pro`; the returned job record reports `nano_banana_2`.

`celestial-sanctuary-original.png` preserves the generated original. The website asset is `../../public/images/celestial-sanctuary.webp`, derived by a non-creative resize and WebP encoding in the Higgsfield sandbox. No new image elements are fabricated during encoding.

Generation uses existing authorized tool access; no plan upgrade or credit purchase was performed. The site remains a local prototype.
